## 8. Training Plans (TIER B)

> Common conventions across plans: all use %HRmax anchors (see Z-1). "Cross training" = cycling, ergometer, swimming at recovery pace. Strengthening ~20–30 min (Ch.10) covering all muscle groups; running drills ≥6 (Ch.11); flexibility/stretching after extensive/recovery runs (Ch.9). Medical check (stress ECG + echocardiogram) required before starting. Prereq: several years of marathon training and basic conditioning completed.

### Plan Index & Prerequisites

**PL-1 — 50 km entry level**
- Prereq: current marathon ~5:00 h. 3–4 days/wk, 4–9 h/wk. Weekly mileage 40–87 km. Total incl. race 490 km. 8 weeks (3 build weeks + recovery week pattern, taper wks 7–8).
- Paces: extensive endurance @7:10–7:15 min/km (75–80% HRmax); intervals @6:30 min/km (up to 90% HRmax).
- Signature workouts: interval progression 4×1 km → 2×2 km → 2×3 km → 3×2 km → 3×3 km (2 km warm-up/down; jog recovery 600 m–2 km); long runs building 25→30→35→37→42 km.
- Race day (Wk8 Sun): 50 km @7:20 min/km = 6:06:40 h. Author's note: at 7:10 min/km → 5:58:20.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:beginner metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.5]

**PL-2 — 50 km sub-5:00 (6:00 min/km)**
- Prereq: marathon ~4:00 h. 3–5 days/wk, 5–10 h/wk. Mileage 49–89 km. Total incl. race 540 km. 8 wks.
- Paces: intensive @5:35 (80–85%); tempo @5:20 (85–90%); extensive @5:50 (75–80%); intervals @5:10 (>90%).
- Race day: 50 km @6:00 min/km = 5:00:00. Author would run 5:50 splits → 4:51:40.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:beginner metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.6]

**PL-3 — 50 km sub-4:30 (5:24 min/km)**
- Prereq: marathon 3:30–3:40 h. 4–5 days/wk, 6–10 h/wk. Mileage 71–108 km. Total 694.4 km. Stretch 3×/wk.
- Paces: intensive @5:05 (80–85%); tempo @4:55 (85–90%); extensive @5:20 (75–80%); intervals @4:45 (>90%).
- Race day: 50 km @5:24 = 4:30:00. Author would run 5:20 splits → 4:26:40.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:intermediate metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.7]

**PL-4 — 50 km sub-4:00 (4:48 min/km) — threshold-raced**
- IMPORTANT distinction: unlike PL-1–3, the race is NOT run at extensive pace — it is run at the aerobic/anaerobic threshold, like a marathon. Requires accurate self-assessment, HR watch, good sleep, low stress.
- Prereq: marathon 3:15 h. 5 days/wk, 7–11 h/wk. Mileage 75–124 km. Total 759.6 km. Strength ≥30 min; stretch 3×/wk.
- Paces: intensive @4:45 (80–85%); tempo @4:35 (85–90%); extensive @4:55 (75–80%); intervals @4:15 (>90%); recovery @5:30 (up to 70%).
- Signature: intervals up to 3×5 km; long runs to 42 km with 5–15 km final acceleration at/≤5 s/km faster than race pace.
- Race day: 50 km @4:48 min/km @80% HRmax.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:advanced system:threshold metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.8]

**PL-5 — 100 km sub-11 h (6:36 min/km)**
- Prereq: marathon 3:44 h. 4–5 days/wk, 7–15 h/wk. Mileage 68–131 km. Total 754 km.
- Paces: intensive @5:55 (80–85%); tempo @5:42 (85–90%); extensive @6:30 (75–80%); intervals @5:30 (>90%).
- Signature: intervals to 3×3 km; long runs 30→50 km; option "training ultramarathon over 50 km at extensive pace."
- Race day: 100 km @6:35 min/km = 10:58:20. Author: 6:30 splits → 10:50.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:intermediate metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.9]

**PL-6 — 100 km sub-10 h (6:00 min/km)**
- Prereq: marathon 3:29 h. 4–6 days/wk, 7–15 h/wk. Mileage 80–139 km. Total 862 km.
- Paces: intensive @5:20 (80–85%); tempo @5:00 (85–90%); extensive @5:50 (75–80%); intervals @4:45 (>90%); recovery jog @6:15 (up to 70%).
- Signature: long runs to 60 km; interval 4×2 km / 3×3 km.
- Race day: 100 km @5:58 min/km = 9:56:40. Author: 5:50 splits → 9:43:20.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:advanced metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.10]

**PL-7 — 6-hour race**
- Prereq: marathon ~5:00 h. 5 days/wk, 7–14 h/wk (train 4–7.5 h/wk). Total time incl. race 43.25 h. Prescribed in **time**, not distance (so the runner can keep moving continuously).
- Structure: extensive endurance runs 60–100 min (80% HRmax); recovery jogs to 240 min (70%); easy interval sets e.g. 3×5, 4×5 min @85–90% HRmax w/ 5 min jog; stabilizing/flexibility 45–60 min on off days.
- Race (Wk8 Sun): 360 min @70–80% HRmax. Expected: **45–65 km in 6 h**.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:beginner metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.11.1]

**PL-8 — 12-hour race**
- Prereq: 6–7 h split-time achievable. 4 days/wk, 7–14 h/wk (train 6.35–15.5 h/wk). Total incl. race 81.83 h. Time-based.
- Structure: extensive runs 60–120 min (80%); long recovery jogs to 420 min (70%); intervals building 8×5 → 10×5 → 6×10 → 8×10 → 10×10 → 8×15 min @85–90% HRmax w/ 3–10 min jog; stabilizing/flexibility 45–60 min.
- Race (Wk8 Sun): 720 min. Expected: **80–110 km in 12 h**.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:intermediate metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.11.2]

**PL-9 — 24-hour race and beyond (180+ km goal)**
- Prereq: 100 km sub-10 h achievable before starting. 5–6 days/wk, 7–20 h/wk. Mileage 80–205 km. Total incl. race 1,110 km. Based on athletes the author knows (not personal experience); targets typically well beyond 230 km/24 h for those athletes, plan here targets 180+ km.
- Paces (by HR): intensive 80–85%; tempo 85–90%; extensive 75–80%; long recovery jog up to 70%.
- Structure: back-to-back long recovery jogs on weekends building to 50 km + 60 km (Wk6 = 200 km week). Recovery week (Wk4) = 80 km.
- Race (Wk8 Sat+Sun): 24-h race at up to 70% HRmax, ≥180 km target. Author's race approach: start ~6:30 min/km, keep HR ≤70% (notes it's actually hard to reach 70% late in the race).
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:advanced metric:hr
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.12]

**PL-10 — Multi-day race (example: 5-day, 298 km race week)**
- Prereq: several years marathon training; good basic conditioning; occasional "double-decker" (2 marathons/ultras on a weekend).
- Aim: for those who just want to finish; successful multi-day runners use similar mileage but different tempos. 4–6 days/wk, 10–25 h/wk. Mileage 70–210 km. Total incl. race 1,173 km.
- Design notes: tempo deliberately slow; only one deliberate-tempo workout (fartlek, athlete-regulated). Core high-mileage weeks 4 (210 km, split into AM/PM doubles) and 6 (160 km). Injury prevention emphasized (shin splints/orthopedic) via antagonist strengthening (Ch.9, Ch.10). Sunday scheduled as rest for family/recovery. Plan also suits 24-h prep → "respectable result beyond 160 km."
- Race week example stages: Lindlar 55 km / Solingen 75 km / Remscheid 58 km / Kürten 65 km / Wipperfürth 45 km, then "Sleep!"
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:advanced phase:race
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.17.13]

**PL-11 — K78 Swiss Alpine Marathon (79 km / 2,370 m gain)**
- Prereq: marathon ~3:30 h. 5 days/wk, 7–14 h/wk. Weekly mileage 68–116 km. Total incl. race ~693 km.
- Trail-specific structure: extensive endurance runs (80% HRmax); **step running** sessions building 20→30→40→50→60→70→80 min (up to/exceeding 90% HRmax) — a step-running session may replace one run; **uphill reps** 6→8→10→12→14→16→18 × 200 m (up to 90% HRmax, jog back 200 m); whole-body strengthening 30 min; whole-body stretching 30 min; long recovery jog (70% HRmax) on hilly terrain building 30→35→40→45→50 km (if flat, use bridges/steps for up-and-down).
- Race (Wk8 Sat): K78, 79.1 km / 49.15 miles.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:advanced metric:hr system:neuromuscular
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.18]

**PL-12 — Western States 100 Miles (100 mi / ~5,500 m gain)**
- Prereq: 100 km in 10 h. 5 days/wk, 8–17 h/wk (excl. race week). Mileage 86–150 km (excl. race week). Total incl. race 975 km.
- Trail-specific structure: extensive endurance runs (80%); step running building 60→70→80→90→100→110→120 min (up to 90% HRmax); uphill reps 14→16→18→20→22→24→25 × 200 m (up to 90% HRmax); strengthening 30 min; stretching 30 min; long recovery jog (70%) on hilly terrain building 40→45→55→60→70 km.
- Race (Wk8 Sat–Sun): Western States 100 miles / 161 km.
- tags: sport:running phase:build type:plan level:advanced metric:hr system:neuromuscular
- [SRC: Ultramarathon Training | 2012 | Ch.18]
