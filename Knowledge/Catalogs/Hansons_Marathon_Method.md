They illustrate what a session designed under the author's principles can look like -- never treat this as an exhaustive list to pick from, and never reproduce one verbatim as your own prescription. ## 8. Structured Plans / Programs

### PLAN-1 — Just Finish Program
- **For:** goal = finish; new runner; never run a marathon; no SOS experience; want simple effective plan.
- Structure: several run days/wk, mileage significantly less than Beginner; less intense, slow careful buildup; controlled mileage & intensity (mostly easy runs).
- Key rules: run easy/conversational for most runs; build time on feet (intensity secondary); if you feel like running hard, do it then keep next day easy; NOT a walk/run program (walk breaks permitted as a teaching tool); should handle 10 mi/wk of running at start.

### PLAN-2 — Beginner Program
- **For:** performance-minded; time goal; first marathon but experienced at other distances; moderate–higher mileage; marathon experience but new to SOS.
- Starts 15 mi/wk → peak upper 50s.
- Phase layout: first 5 weeks all easy (base building, no SOS); week 6 introduces SOS (speed + tempo); then marathon-specific block (strength replaces speed, tempo & long runs lengthen); final ~10-day taper.
- Mileage jump wk 5 (~25 mi) → wk 6 (~41 mi) due to SOS start + loss of Monday rest/crosstrain. Optional smoothing: substitute 4–5 mi (6–8 km) easy Monday; do 8×400 instead of 12×400 Tuesday → wk5 ~29 mi, wk6 ~38 mi.
- Long-run buildup: gradual to 10 mi, then 15–16 mi alternating with 10 mi.
- Speed at 5K/10K pace; tempo at GMP; strength at MP−10.
- Total ~700 miles over 18 weeks (basis for needing 2 pairs of shoes).
- Full schedule: [IMAGE 3-1/3-2/3-3.jpg — image-only]. WU/CD 1 mi (2 km) each for Speed/Strength/Tempo included in weekly mileage.

### PLAN-3 — Advanced Program
- **For:** performance-oriented; marathon experience; experience running higher mileage. Best for ≥1 prior marathon (or first-timer accustomed to 50+ mi/wk).
- Higher mileage from wk 1; peak just over 60 mi (60–70).
- Extra mileage comes from longer weekday EASY runs, not more SOS.
- Speed begins first full week; tempo second full week. Long run every OTHER week (16 mi max). A 10-mi run = "easy" in Advanced (vs "long" in Beginner).
- Full schedule: [IMAGE 4-1/4-2/4-3.jpg — image-only]. WU/CD 1 mi (2 km) each included in weekly mileage.

### PLAN-4 — Elite Program (Hansons-Brooks Distance Project) — nine-day cycle
```
Sunday: Long, 18–20 mi
Monday: a.m. Easy 12–14 mi; p.m. Easy 4–6 mi
Tuesday: same as Monday
Wednesday: Tempo run, 8–12 mi
Thursday: same as Monday
Friday: same as Monday
Saturday: Track, 5 mi of speed (20 sec faster than marathon pace)
Sunday: same as Monday
Monday: same as previous Monday
```
- Weekly volume: men 120–140 mi/wk (marathon) / 100–120 (shorter races); women 120–130 mi/wk.
- Marathon training block: 12–14 weeks (few weeks return-to-running + 8–10 wk high mileage/intense + few weeks taper).
- Easy runs during marathon peak ≈ 6:00–6:30/mi; long runs ≈ 5:30–6:00/mi; if GMP = 5:05/mi, most runs 25 sec–1:15/mi slower.
- SOS as % of weekly volume (proportionate across programs): long run ~16–17%; speed ~5% (6 mi); strength ~8–10% (9–12 mi); tempo ~12% (10 mi).
- Support factors: naps, 8–10 hr sleep/night, shoe supply from Brooks, access to chiropractors/PTs.

## 9. Discrete Protocol Library

### P-1 — Easy Run
- Goal / target outcome: aerobic development (mitochondria, capillaries, fat-burning), active recovery, injury prevention via gentle overload.
- Domain / context: endurance-sport
- Total duration: 20 min – 2.5 hr
- Structure: continuous easy running.
- Intensity/dose anchors: 55–75% VO2max; GMP + 1:00–2:00 min/mi. "Fast easy" = GMP+1:00; "slow easy" = GMP+2:00.
- When used: daily backbone; slow end for WU/CD & day after SOS; faster end day after tempo / 2nd easy day before long run; Saturday can be faster if feeling good.
- Execution notes: truly easy; run too slow only breaks down tendon/bone without aerobic benefit; don't habitually run faster (injury risk).
- tags: type:protocol sport:running system:aerobic metric:pace subtopic:easy-run level:general-population
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 3]

### P-2 — Long Run
- Goal: endurance, glycogen storage, fat utilization, capillary growth, mental coping; simulate LAST 16 mi of marathon.
- Domain / context: endurance-sport
- Total duration: 2:00–3:00 hr optimal window
- Structure: continuous easy-to-moderate run; max 16 mi (standard programs).
- Intensity/dose anchors: easy-to-moderate; 25–30% of weekly mileage.
- When used: Sundays; increase ≤2 mi/wk; alternate high/low every 2 weeks.
- Progressions/variations (Elite): Steady-State Effort; Last 3 GO!; Depletion (see P-9, P-10, P-11).
- Execution notes: beginners err easy; advanced hold moderate. Exceeding ~2 hr → muscle breakdown, diminishing returns.
- tags: type:protocol sport:running system:aerobic subtopic:long-run level:general-population metric:pace phase:build
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 3]
- [EVIDENCE: benchmark] (Daniels 25–30%/2:30–3:00; Martin 90 min–2 hr/18–25 mi elites; Vigil 2:00–3:00 hr; Noakes 70–85% VO2max ≥2 hr; Costill 50% glycogen at 2 hr)

### P-3 — Speed Workout (intervals)
- Goal: VO2max, AT, running economy, myoglobin, mental toughness, pacing baseline.
- Domain / context: endurance-sport
- Structure: WU 1.5–3 mi → repeats at 5K/10K pace with jog recoveries → CD 1.5–3 mi. Total fast running = 3 mi.
- Intensity/dose anchors: 5K or 10K goal pace; 80–95% VO2max; just under 100% VO2max.
- Interval duration: optimal 2–8 min per rep (400 m ~2 min for beginner; too short wastes time, too long causes lactate buildup).
- Recovery: 50–100% of repeat duration (e.g., 2-min rep → 1–2 min recovery); beginners get longer recovery initially; shortened with fitness. "If too tired to jog recovery, running too hard."
- Example decoded: 2-mi WU / 6×800 m @ 5K pace w/ 400-m jog recovery / 2-mi CD.
- When used: early in program.
- Sequence: 12×400 → 8×600 → 6×800 → 5×1000 → 4×1200 → 3×1600 (Advanced ladder up then down).
- Execution notes: never exceed 100% VO2max pace (structural muscle breakdown, anaerobic reliance, injury). If can't reach 3 mi total, running too hard.
- tags: type:protocol sport:running system:vo2max system:threshold subtopic:speed-intervals level:intermediate metric:pace phase:build
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 3, 4]

### P-4 — Strength Workout
- Goal: raise AT/fractional utilization, lactate tolerance & clearance, running economy; adapt to marathon fatigue; maintain VO2max.
- Domain / context: endurance-sport
- Structure: WU 1.5–3 mi → longer repeats (e.g., 6×1 mi) with SHORT jog recovery → CD 1.5–3 mi. Total = ~6 mi higher-intensity running (double speed volume).
- Intensity/dose anchors: **GMP − 10 sec/mi (MP−10)**; 60–80% VO2max; near half-marathon pace for faster runners.
- Recovery: fraction of repeat duration (e.g., 6×1-mi → ¼-mi jog ≈ 2:30–3:00 at 8:00 pace, <50% of interval).
- When used: marathon-specific phase (after speed block).
- Example impact: GMP 8:00 → 3:30 finish; 7:50 (MP−10) → 3:25 finish.
- Execution notes: don't exceed prescribed pace (adaptations occur at that speed). Use marked bike path/loop; avoid monotonous track.
- tags: type:protocol sport:running system:threshold subtopic:strength-run level:intermediate metric:pace phase:build
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 3 — Table 3.4 image-only]

### P-5 — Tempo Workout
- Goal: internalize/practice race pace; running economy at GMP; endurance; nutrition/gear dress rehearsal; goal-validation.
- Domain / context: endurance-sport
- Structure: WU 1–2 mi → continuous run at GMP → CD 1–2 mi. Tempo distance progresses: Beginner 5→8 mi; Advanced 6,7,8,9,10 mi (10 mi at GMP; total 12–14 mi w/ WU/CD).
- Intensity/dose anchors: exactly goal marathon pace (below AT).
- When used: added ~same time as speed (Beginner wk6; Advanced 2nd week); continues through program before long run (cumulative fatigue).
- Execution notes: never hammer a tempo because it feels easy (compromises gains + pace-internalization). If struggling to hold GMP for long tempos → goal may be too aggressive.
- tags: type:protocol sport:running system:threshold system:aerobic subtopic:tempo-run level:general-population metric:pace phase:build
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 3]

### P-6 — Dynamic Warm-Up Level 1 (DWU1) — pre-easy/long run, ≤10 min
After 1–3 mi WU, perform any order: (1) Arm Swings 6–10 reps circular + 6–10 across-chest; (2) Side Bends 16–20; (3) Hip Circles 10–12 each direction; (4) Half-Squat 10–12; (5) Leg Kicks 10–12 each leg (pendulum); (6) Leg Swings 10–12 each leg (across body).
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:dynamic-warmup level:general-population sport:running system:neuromuscular
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 7]

### P-7 — Dynamic Warm-Up Level 2 (DWU2) — pre-SOS, add to DWU1, ≤20 min total
(1) Slow Skipping 30–50 m (10–15 s); (2) High Knees 30–50 m; (3) Butt Kicks 30–50 m; (4) Cariocas/grapevine 30–50 m; (5) Bounders 30–50 m; (6) Sprints 4–6 × 75–100 m near-maximal (≤15 s; slower runners start 75 m; run with wind, jog back against wind).
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:dynamic-warmup level:intermediate sport:running system:neuromuscular
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 7]

### P-8 — Light Static Stretching (LS) — post-run
9 stretches, hold 20 sec, 1–3 reps each, ~10–15 min; never before a run; not to point of pain: (1) Low Back; (2) Shoulder; (3) Chest; (4) Calves; (5) Gluteal; (6) Groin; (7) Hamstrings; (8) Hip Flexors/Quads; (9) Hips.
- Tissue-lengthening variant: hold 3–5 min to slight discomfort; only for Achilles, hip flexors, maybe hamstrings; ~12 weeks to see change; do several hours after (not immediately after) intense workout.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:static-stretching level:general-population sport:running
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 7, 9]

### P-9 — Elite Long Run: Steady-State Effort
- Goal: stimulate aerobic threshold, fat-burning; mental focus.
- Structure: begin as normal long run → increase to ~30 sec/mi slower than GMP, hold for 50–75% of the run.
- Execution: precise pacing; too hard = premature wall, too slow = miss effect.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:steady-state level:elite sport:running system:fatoxidation metric:pace
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Appendix A] — NOTE: not for Beginner/Advanced.

### P-10 — Elite Long Run: Last 3 GO!
- Goal: run fast on tired legs; recruit intermediate/fast-twitch fibers.
- Structure: moderate pace throughout; last 3 mi at GMP or faster.
- Note: useful for advanced runners chasing qualifiers.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:last-3-go level:elite sport:running system:neuromuscular
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Appendix A]

### P-11 — Elite Long Run: Depletion
- Goal: burn most glycogen → trigger greater glycogen storage before taper.
- Structure: long run at slower pace; NO food before, only water throughout. Used once or twice per cycle; as last significant long run before taper.
- Caution: abandons normal fueling practice — used sparingly only.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:depletion-run level:elite sport:running system:fatoxidation
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Appendix A]

### P-12 — Elite Tempo: The Cutdown
- 10–12 mi; start 6:00/mi (elite men, within easy range) decreasing 10 sec/mi down to half-marathon pace. Example: 6:00, 6:00, 5:50, 5:40, 5:30, 5:20, 5:10, 5:00, 4:50.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:cutdown level:elite sport:running metric:pace
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Appendix A]

### P-13 — Elite Tempo: Five and GO!
- 10-mi tempo: first 5 mi at marathon pace; second 5 mi at half-marathon pace or as fast as possible.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:five-and-go level:elite sport:running metric:pace
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Appendix A]

### P-14 — Elite: The Simulator
- 26.2 KILOMETERS at marathon goal pace on terrain matching the goal race. Total ~20 mi with WU/CD. ~4 weeks before goal marathon; first of a series of hard workouts.
- Requirement: ≥100 mi/wk; matching course. Bad idea for low-mileage runners.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:simulator level:elite sport:running metric:pace phase:peak
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Appendix A]

### P-15 — Elite Strength: Bread-and-Butter (2 × 6 miles)
- After WU: first 6 mi at GMP − 5 sec/mi; then 10 min light jog; second 6 mi at GMP − 5 to 10 sec/mi.
- Scheduled 10 days after the Simulator; <3 weeks before race day; marks start of Elite taper.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:bread-and-butter level:elite sport:running metric:pace phase:peak
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Appendix A]

### P-16 — Strength: Body-Weight & Movement (BWM)
(1) Crunch — 3×10 progressing to 3×25 (ball variation); (2) Back Extension 12–15 (ball variation); (3) Superman 12–15/side; (4) Squat (deep, glutes/hamstrings); (5) Bridge 12–15, hold 1–3 s (one-leg progression); (6) Side Plank hold 10–20 s each side.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:bwm-strength level:general-population sport:running system:neuromuscular
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 7]

### P-17 — Strength: Resistance Training (RT)
1–3 sets × 10–12 reps; last reps should reach form failure; ~60 s rest between sets; controlled movements. Upper: (1) Chest Press; (2) Lat Pull-Down; (3) Kneeling Row. Lower: (4) Dumbbell Deadlift; (5) Walking Lunges 10–12/leg; (6) Side Lunges 12/side.
- Progression: master movement work ≥2 weeks → add RT 1×/wk on easy/off day (continue BWM 1–2 days/wk) → after 3 weeks RT 2×/wk. If <10 weeks to race, don't progress beyond movement work.
- tags: type:protocol subtopic:resistance-training level:intermediate sport:running system:neuromuscular
- [SRC: HMM | 2nd ed., 2016 | Ch. 7]
