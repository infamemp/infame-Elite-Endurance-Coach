They illustrate what a session designed under the author's principles can look like -- never treat this as an exhaustive list to pick from, and never reproduce one verbatim as your own prescription. ## 8. Training Plans (TIER B)

> Notation: RBI = Rest Between Intervals; RBS = Rest Between Sets. "3 × 8 min SS (5 min RBI)" = 3 SteadyState intervals of 8 min, 5 min recovery between. OU notation "3 × 12 OU (2U,1O), 5 min RBI" = 3 intervals of 12 min alternating 2 min Under / 1 min Over, 5 min recovery between intervals. TL notation "TL 1/3/5" = ThresholdLadder = 1 min PI, 3 min CR, 5 min SS. All rides are EnduranceMiles (EM) base with the intervals embedded within the stated total duration.

For riders <5 yrs experience; crits, XC/short-track MTB, cyclocross, road races ≤~60 mi. Best performance weekend = week 8. Weeks 9–11 = hold fitness. (Similar to plan Rutberg used with John Fallon.)

### PLAN-03 — Cyclocross (Table 6.3)
Ramps to PI mainstay quickly; often 1 long set. Leverages summer fitness. Start racing as early as week 4. TL 1/3/5 = 1 min PI, 3 min CR, 5 min SS; TL 2/4/6 = 2 min PI, 4 min CR, 6 min SS. Run-up: during last 15 sec of PI dismount, run up hill/stairs, remount to complete PI.

| Wk | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 3×8 SS (4 RBI) | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 3×8 SS (4 RBI) | Rest | Group or 90–120 EM | 90–120 EM (hilly) |
| 2 | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 3×9 TL 1/3/5 (5 RBI) | Rest | 75–90 EM w/ 3×10 SS (5 RBI) | Rest | 30–45 'cross practice + group/90–120 EM | 90–120 EM or group |
| 3 | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 3×9 TL 1/3/5 (5 RBI) | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 3×9 TL 1/3/5 (5 RBI) | Rest | 90 EM w/ 3×12 SS (5 RBI) | 30–45 'cross practice + group/90–120 EM |
| 4 | Rest | Rest or 30 easy 'cross practice | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 3×2 PI (2 RBI) | Rest | Race or 30–45 'cross practice + 60–90 EM w/ 8×2 PI (2 RBI) | 120–150 EM or group |
| 5 | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 8×2 PI (2 RBI) | 60–90 EM w/ 15×1 PI (1 RBI) | Rest | Rest | Race or 30–45 'cross practice + 90 EM w/ 3×12 OU (1O,1U) (6 RBI) | 90–150 EM or group |
| 6 | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 5×2 PI (2 RBI), 8 easy, 5×1 PI w/ Run-up (1 RBI) | 60–90 EM w/ 15×1 PI (1 RBI) | Rest | Rest | Race or 30–45 'cross practice + 90–120 EM w/ 3×12 TL 2/4/6 (6 RBI) | 90–120 EM or group |
| 7 | Rest | Rest or 30 easy 'cross practice | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 5×2 PI (2 RBI), 8 easy, 5×1 PI w/ Run-up (1 RBI) | Rest | Race or 30–45 'cross practice + 90–120 EM w/ 3×12 TL 2/4/6 (6 RBI) | 120–150 EM or group |
| 8 | Rest | 60–90 EM on 'cross bike w/ 10×1 PI (1 RBI) | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 5×1 PI (1 RBI), 8 easy, 3×6 OU (1O,1U) (3 RBI) | Rest | Race | 120–150 EM w/ 3×12 OU (1O,1U) (6 RBI) |

- Additional tips: race often; train on 'cross bike as much as possible (swap power meter); use clinics for skills (ref: Simon Burney, *Cyclocross Training and Technique*).

### PLAN-07 — Ultraendurance (Gravel / Ultra MTB) (Table 8.1)
12-week plan. Prerequisite: complete a Competitor or Century program + 4-week recovery block first; must be experienced. Realistic goal: ~10 hr Leadville 100 finish (sub-9 a stretch). Requires ≥7 hr/week; peaks at ~13.5–14.5 hr in week 10. Weekday workouts as short as 90 min. Mon/Fri rest days critical.

| 5 | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 3×12 OU (2U,1O) (6 RBI) | 60–90 EM w/ 3×12 OU (2U,1O) (6 RBI) | Opt: 45 recovery | Rest | 180 EM w/ 4×15 SS (8 RBI) | 180 EM or group |

- Road bike preferred for midweek intervals (time, terrain, weather); Sat/Sun on MTB from week 10 to race day.

### PLAN-08 — Maintenance Program (4-week, Table 5.1)
Used in the "OFF"/between-cycles period. EM-focused; reintroduce structure gradually.

| Wk | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rest | Rest | Rest | 45–60 easy spinning | Rest | Group or 90–120 EM | 90–120 EM hilly |
| 2 | Rest | 60–90 EM | Rest | 75–90 EM w/ 15 min upper-EM | Rest | Group or 90–120 EM w/ 15–20 min upper-EM | 90–120 EM hilly |
| 3 | Rest | 60–90 EM | Rest | 75–90 EM w/ 20 min upper-EM | Rest | Group or 120–150 EM w/ 30–40 min upper-EM | 90–120 EM hilly |
| 4 | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 10–20 min SS | Rest | 60–90 EM w/ 6–8 PI-type efforts | Rest | Group or 120–150 EM w/ 30–40 min upper-EM | 120–150 EM or group |

- Detailed "OFF" period FAQ guidance: Week 1 = recovery (no riding Mon–Wed; Thu 45–60 min light spin; weekend normal volume, no structure). Weeks 1–2 back riding: primarily EM, 20 min near top of EM range on 60–90 min ride, 30 min on 2+ hr ride. Weeks 3–4: efforts at/above LT, no structure (group rides, climbs). Weeks 5–6: as weeks 3–4, keep volume up at EM intensity.
