> **This is a catalog, not a menu.** These are worked examples and named case studies from *Daniels' Running Formula (Jack Daniels)*, included for calibration only. They illustrate what a session designed under the author's principles can look like -- never treat this as an exhaustive list to pick from, and never reproduce one verbatim as your own prescription. Binding physiological targets, ratios, and constraints live in `Knowledge/Principles/Jack_Daniels_Running_Formula.md`.

- Wk 1–2: 25 mi/wk + 4 × 8 ST sessions/wk
