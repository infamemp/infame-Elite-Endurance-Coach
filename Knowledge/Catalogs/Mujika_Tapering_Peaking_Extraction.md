### Martin Fiz: Sample Interval Training Sets for Tapering (Marathon)

```
• 3 × 4,000 m: 2:55 min/km with 3 min recovery jog
• 25 × 400 m: 1:10 min with 45 s recovery jog
• 15 × 1,000 m: 2:55 min/km with 1 min recovery jog
• 5 × 3,000 m: 2:57 min/km with 2 min recovery jog
• 70 min continuous run, increasing pace from 3:40 min/km to 3:10 min/km
```

Key principle: recovery times are shorter during the taper vs. race pacing sets, with faster running. "Recovery times are key."

### Jason Queally: 3-Day Training Cycle During Taper (Sprint Track Cycling)

**Cycle Option A:**
```
Day 1: Flying 100 and 200 m efforts × 2, then 2–3 standing-start quarter laps
Day 2: Standing-start 500 m × 3
Day 3: Track intervals (flying 500 m × 4 reps × 2 sets, rolling 750 m recovery) + flying 1 km × 2
```

**Cycle Option B:**
```
Day 1: Flying 100 and 200 m × 2, then 2 motorbike-paced efforts (one lap)
Day 2: Standing-start 500 m × 3
Day 3: Turbo interval type 1: 30 s at 800 W / 60 s recovery × 4 reps × 2 sets;
        Turbo interval type 2 (Tabata-derived): 20 s at 650–700 W / 10 s recovery × 7 reps × 2 sets
```

Training is maximal effort; the day between training blocks is complete rest. Queally monitors recovery subjectively (mood state, willingness to train) and objectively (timing gates, SRM power meter).

### South African Rugby Springboks: RWC 2007 Taper Principles

Used exponential taper with fast time constant of decay after pool games for quarterfinals, semifinals, and final. Primary aim was minimizing cumulative fatigue between consecutive games, not achieving further physiological adaptations. Recovery protocols applied throughout the year:

| Period | Recovery Guidelines |
|--------|---------------------|
| Beginning of week | Body weight measurement; fluid monitoring; flexibility; massage; high protein/carbohydrate intake; individual nutrition plan |
| Middle of week (main session) | High protein; hydration; rest; stay off feet; ≥8 hr sleep |
| End of week | Light flexibility; massage; high fluid/carbohydrate; rest; ≥8 hr sleep |
| Pregame and game | High fluid/carbohydrate; fluid during game; compulsory ice jackets at halftime; bench thermo suits; ≥8 hr sleep |
| Postgame | High fluid/carbohydrate; light locomotion; light flexibility; cold immersion; avoid alcohol |

Weekly fatigue monitoring used subjective scales: training intensity 0–10 scale and perceived fatigue 1–10 scale [Table 12.2 — extractable above].
