# Cycling Training Zones Reference Database

This file contains standardized intensity training zones for cycling across major coaching methodologies (Friel, Coggan, Carmichael).

## Methodology: Friel Cycling Zones
* **Sport:** Cycling
* **Primary Metrics:** Power, Functional Threshold Heart Rate (FTHR)
* **Intensity Anchors:** % FTP, % FTHR

| Zone ID | Zone Name | % FTP Range | % FTHR Range | RPE (1-10) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Zone 1 | Active Recovery | < 55% | < 81% | 1–2 |
| Zone 2 | Endurance | 55–74% | 82–89% | 3–4 |
| Zone 3 | Tempo | 75–89% | 90–93% | 5–6 |
| Zone 4 | Lactate Threshold | 90–104% | 94–99% | 7 |
| Zone 5 / 5a | VO2max / Threshold Aerobic | 105–120% | 100–102% | 8 |
| Zone 6 / 5b | Anaerobic Capacity | > 120% | 103–106% | 9 |
| Zone 7 / 5c | Neuromuscular Power | > 150% | > 107% | 10 |
