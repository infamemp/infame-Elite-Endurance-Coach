##### Joe Friel Training Zones (Cycling)
| Zone / Workout & Intensity | Short Description | Usage & Training Benefits | Suggested Race Intensities | Suggested RPE (Borg 1–10) |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **Zone 1** <55% FTP / <81% FTHR | Active Recovery | Used for easy recovery rides between hard workouts to shed fatigue and allow the body to adapt. | N/A | **[RPE 1–2]**Very easy recovery |
| **Zone 2** 55–74% FTP / 82–89% FTHR | Aerobic Endurance | Builds the aerobic foundation, improves capillary beds, and trains the body to use oxygen efficiently. | Ultra-distance / General endurance | **[RPE 3–4]**Comfortable |
| **Zone 3** 75–89% FTP / 90–93% FTHR | Tempo | Develops early muscular endurance; utilizes long, steady efforts slightly below the anaerobic threshold. | Steady pacing in long road races | **[RPE 5–6]**Moderate to somewhat hard |
| **Zone 4** 90–104% FTP / 94–99% FTHR | Muscular Endurance | Builds the anaerobic threshold; involves long intervals designed to sustain high-intensity efforts. | Time Trials, sustained long climbs | **[RPE 7–8]**Hard, redlining effort |
| **Zone 5** 105–120% FTP / 100–106% FTHR | Anaerobic Endurance | Uses short intervals (seconds to minutes) to maximize aerobic capacity and stroke volume. | Criteriums, breakaways, short steep climbs | **[RPE 9]**Very hard |
| **Zone 6** 120–150% FTP / >107% FTHR | Anaerobic Capacity | Very brief, high-intensity surges to build top-end power and neuromuscular force. | Short bursts and attacks in road races | **[RPE 9–10]**Near maximal |
| **Zone 7** &gt;150% FTP | Sprint Power | Maximal sprints (under 20 seconds) with long recoveries to develop explosive racing power. | Finishing sprints | **[RPE 10]**All-out maximal effort |

##### Andrew Coggan Classic Training Levels (Cycling)
| Zone / Workout & Intensity | Short Description | Usage & Training Benefits | Suggested Race Intensities | Suggested RPE (Borg 1–10) |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **Level 1** <55% FTP / <68% FTHR | Active Recovery | Flushes built-up waste products and maintains muscle suppleness without adding fatigue. | N/A | **[RPE <2]**Very easy |
| **Level 2** 56–75% FTP / 69–83% FTHR | Endurance | Builds base endurance, strengthens the heart muscle, increases mitochondrial levels, and develops capillaries. | Ironman / Ultra-distance | **[RPE 2–3]**Comfortable |
| **Level 3** 76–90% FTP / 84–94% FTHR | Tempo | Develops a strong aerobic engine and sustained rhythmic power. | Road races under 2.5 hours, Half-Ironman | **[RPE 3–4]**Moderate |
| **Level 4** 91–105% FTP / 95–105% FTHR | Lactate Threshold | Increases power at lactate threshold, improving your ability to sustain high-intensity efforts. | Criteriums, circuit races, Olympic triathlons | **[RPE 4–5]**Hard |
| **Level 5** 106–120% FTP / >106% FTHR | VO2max | Elicits improvements in maximal oxygen uptake (VO2max), stroke volume, and cardiac output. | Shorter time trials, race-winning breakaways | **[RPE 6–7]**Very hard |
| **Level 6** 121–150% FTP | Anaerobic Capacity | Short intervals (30s to 3 min) to build anaerobic capacity and lactate tolerance. | Track pursuits, BMX, short steep hill climbs | **[RPE >7]**Extremely hard |
| **Level 7** Maximal | Neuromuscular Power | Very short (under 30 sec) all-out sprints to increase explosive power and fast-twitch muscle hypertrophy. | Finishing sprints, standing starts | **[RPE 10]**All-out effort |
